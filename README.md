# Shakshuka Tycoon

**You don't cook the shakshuka. You keep the lights on.**

![Shakshuka Tycoon concept sketch](images/screen-sketch.svg)

Shakshuka Tycoon is a restaurant business game for PC and the browser. You open a tiny shakshuka place with ₪20,000 and try to grow it into three branches within a year without going broke.

You never touch a frying pan. You set the prices, order eggs and tomatoes before they spoil, hire cooks and waiters, and make sure there's money in the bank when rent, salaries and taxes are due. A competitor opens across the street, tomato prices jump, and the stove breaks on the busiest day of the week.

It's an educational game that doesn't feel like one. Players pick up income, expenses, profit, cash flow and taxes because they need them to make rent.
